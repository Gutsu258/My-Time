# Welcome to My Time Blog

This is a public dashboard where I track how I spend my study and project time using [Timewarrior](https://timewarrior.net/).

## How It Works

1. I track sessions locally with `timew start <tags>` and `timew stop`
2. Export the data: `timew export > all-time.json`
3. Push to this repo — the dashboard updates automatically

## What You'll Find Here

- **Stats tab** — heatmaps, charts, session logs, and goal tracking
- **Blog tab** — posts about what I'm learning and building
- **Videos tab** — screen recordings and walkthroughs

Feel free to explore the data. Everything is tracked honestly and in real-time.
